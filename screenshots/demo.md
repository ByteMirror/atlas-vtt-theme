# The Hollow Oak Inn

A crooked coaching inn on the old forest road, kept by the widow Marigold Thrupp and her three silent sons.

> The common room smells of woodsmoke and wet wool. A dozen travellers hunch over their bowls, and none of them looks up as you enter.

**Rumours:** The miller has not been seen since the first frost.
**Lodging:** A bed in the loft costs 2 silver pennies a night.

## Encounters on the road

| d6  | Encounter               | Number |
| --- | ----------------------- | ------ |
| 1   | Charcoal burners        | 2d4    |
| 2   | A tinker with a lame ox | 1      |
| 3   | Bog lanterns            | 1d6    |

- **By day:** roll once at noon.
- **By night:** roll at dusk and again at midnight.

---

### The cellar

Barrels line the north wall. The third one is empty and hides a trapdoor.

> [!note] For the referee
> The trapdoor leads to the smugglers' tunnel.

#### Treasure

A pouch of 30 gold pieces and a silver whistle.
